Baguette Sandwich Rolls

★★★★

Bread

Difficulty: Medium Source: Brian Lagerstrom

Ingredients

525g bread flour

12g instant yeast

22g sugar

12g salt

380g warm water

Directions

1. Mix everything together in a bowl and let it stand for 30 minutes to fully hydrate.
2. Pulse in food processor 45 times and then kneed by hand.
3. Transfer to a bowl and round it into a ball by rotating it and tucking 4. Cover and allow to ferment at room temp for 60 min.
5. The dough should have risen a lot. Punch it down to release some gas, then do a strength building fold. Then tuck and round into a ball, cover, and let ferment on at room temp for 60 minutes.
6. The dough should have risen a lot again. Flour dough and work surface and divide it into 6 equal pieces (about 150g each). Preshape each piece into a ball. Once preshaped into balls, cover with a damp towel to relax for 15-20 minutes.
7. Shape baguettes by rolling like a cigar 8 times pressing into itself. and place into a baguette pan.
8. Cover shaped baguettes with damp towel and proof for 45 min
9. Preheat oven to 400 with baking stone/steel on top rack and cast iron skillet on bottom
rack.
10. When baguettes are proofed and ready to bake, they should indent when you poke them, then slowly bounce back.
11. Score diagonally across the top of each. Spray top of loaves liberally with water then load into preheated oven on top of baking stone/steel.
12. Pour boiling water into cast iron skillet on bottom rack and close oven door quickly to bake for 30-35mins. Loaves are done when toasty and golden brown.
