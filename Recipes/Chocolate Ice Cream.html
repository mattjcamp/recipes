Chocolate Ice Cream

★★★★★

Sweets

Source: Cuisinart

Ingredients

1/4 cup Unsweetened Cocoa Powder (Dutch Process Preferred), Original

proportion was 1/2 cup but reduced since last time it was too chocolatey

1/2 cup Granulated Sugar

1/3 cup firmly packed brown sugar

1 cup whole milk

2 cups heavy cream

1/2 tbsp pure vanilla extract

Pinch Salt

Directions

1. Place the cocoa and sugars in a medium bowl, stir to combine

2. Add the whole milk and use a hand mixer on low speed or whisk to combine until the cocoa and sugars are dissolved, about 1 to 2 minutes

3. Stir in the heavy cream and vanilla

4. If not freezing immediately, cover and refrigerate until ready to use

5. Turn machine on; pour mixture into freezer bowl, and let mix until thickened, about 25 to 35 minutes

6. The ice cream will have a soft, creamy texture

7. If a firmer consistency is desired, transfer the ice cream to an airtight container and place in freezer for about 2 hours

8. Remove from freezer about 15 minutes before serving.
