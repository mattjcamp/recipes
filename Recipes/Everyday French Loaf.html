Everyday French Loaf

★★★★

Bread

Prep Time: 24 hours Cook Time: 40 minutes Difficulty: Hard Source: King Arthur Baking

Ingredients

Preferment


214g unbleached flour

1/4 tsp instant yeast

214g water, cold (55°F to 60°F)


Dough


427g (85% of flour) preferment, Can sub sourdough levain

308g (62% of flour) water, warm (110°F)

500g unbleached flour

15g (3% of flour) salt

2g (1/2 tsp) instant yeast

Directions

Night Before Baking: Preferment (12 Hours)

In a medium bowl, combine the flour, yeast (or sourdough starter), and water. Use a big bowl so you can throw the other stuff in the next morning.

Mix until no dry patches of flour remain

Cover and let it rest overnight at room temperature, 12 to 16 hours

Autolyse (20 minutes)

Combine all the ingredients and mix well with a dough whisk

Using a wet hand and a light touch, quickly work the dough against the side of the bowl until it begins to smooth out and is tacky.

Once you’ve mixed your dough, let it rest, covered, for 20 minutes

Bulk Rise: Slap & Folds (40 minutes)

Perform 8-12 bowl folds

Let rest for 20 minutes

Perform another bowl fold

Let rest for 20 minutes

Perform a very gentle coil fold

Bulk Rise: Rest (1 Hour)

Cover and let the dough rest at room temperature for 1 hour, or until it feels puﬀy and marshmallowy.

Shaping (15 minutes)

Use a bowl scraper to gently ease the dough out of the bowl and onto a work surface lightly dusted with flour, then divide the dough into 2 even pieces (650g each)

Gently pre-shape each piece of dough into a round

Cover the pre-shaped dough and let it rest on a lightly floured surface for 15 minutes

Generously dust two 9" x 3" round or oval-proofing baskets with rice flour

Shape each piece of dough into a boule or batard, then place them seam-side up in the prepared baskets

Proof (1 Hour)

Cover the baskets and let the dough rise until it looks and feels marshmallowy and a small indentation remains when pressed with a floured finger, 45 minutes to 1 hour

Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 475°F with the dutch oven on the middle rack and with a baking steel on the lower rack

BAKE

Use a baker’s lame or razor blade to score the tops of the loaves

Load the loaf into the dutch oven

Reduce oven temperature to 450F

Bake, covered, for 20 minutes

Remove cover and bake for another 20 minutes

Bread will be 210F when ready

Turn oﬀ the oven, leave the door open a crack, and leave the bread in the oven for an hour or so to cure

Notes

- Increased original amounts by 1.924% so that the dough will fill the larger baskets I use
- If only using sourdough Levain, you will need to bulk rise for four hours and bench rise for
2 hours
