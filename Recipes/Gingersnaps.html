Gingersnaps

★★★★

Prep Time: 12 minutes Cook Time: 13 minutes Total Time: 30 minutes Difficulty: Medium Source: King Arthur Baking

Ingredients

Dough


3/4 cup (138g) vegetable shortening*

1 cup (198g) granulated sugar

1/2 tsp table salt

2 tsp baking soda

1 large egg

1/3 cup (113g) molasses

2 1/3 cups (280g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

1 to 2 tsp ginger

1/2 tsp cloves

1 tsp cinnamon


Coating


1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar

1 tsp cinnamon

Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F
2. Lightly grease (or line with parchment) two baking sheets
3. Beat together the shortening, sugar, salt, and baking soda
4. Beat in the egg, then the molasses
5. Add the flour and spices, beating to make a smooth, fairly stiﬀ dough
6. To make the coating, combine the sugar and cinnamon, and place in a shallow pan or
dish
7. Drop the dough in 1" balls into the cinnamon-sugar mixture; a teaspoon cookie scoop is
perfect here
8. Roll the balls in the sugar to coat, then transfer them to the prepared baking sheets,
leaving at least 1 1/2" between them; they'll spread as they bake
9. Bake the gingersnaps for 11 minutes, for cookies that are crisp around the edges, and
"bendy" in the center
10. Bake for 13 minutes, for cookies that are crisp/crunchy all the way through
11. Remove the gingersnaps from the oven, and cool right on the pan, or on a rack
12. Cool completely, then store tightly wrapped at room temperature
