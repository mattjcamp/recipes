Chili

★★★★

Prep

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

2 onion

2 green bell pepper

2 cloves garlic

1 lbs ground meat

4 tbsp tomato paste

2 tsp ground cumin

4 tsp chili powder

2 tbsp hot pepper flakes

2 28-ounce cans canned tomatoes

4 15-ounce cans cans kidney beans

Directions

1. Chop garlic, onion and bell pepper
2. Rinse beans
3. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat
4. Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 4 to 6
minutes
5. Add the turkey and cook, breaking up with a spoon, until no longer pink, 3 to 5 minutes
6. Stir in the tomato paste, cumin, and chili pepper. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute
7. Add the tomatoes and their juices, beans, ½ cup water, 1 ¼ teaspoons salt, and ¼
teaspoon pepper. Bring to a boil
8. Reduce heat and simmer, add chili flakes and hot sauce, stirring occasionally, until slightly
thickened, 12 to 15 minutes

Notes

Try the Brian L method of low and slow oven cook instead of stove top
