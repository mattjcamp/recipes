Raising Cane's Sauce

★★★★

Sauces, Weeknights

Prep Time: 5 minutes Cook Time: 5 minutes Total Time: 6 hours Difficulty: Easy Servings: 4 Source: alphafoodie

Ingredients

62g mayonnaise (1/2 cup)

37g ketchup (3 tbsp)

14g Worcestershire sauce

½ tsp garlic powder

¼ tsp black pepper

¼ tsp salt

Directions

Place all the ingredients into a bowl and stir to combine

Once fully mixed, leave in the refrigerator for 6 hours before serving to have the flavors meld together over time, so prepare the sauce a day before serving it for the best overall flavor
