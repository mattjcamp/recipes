French Onion Soup

★★★★

Family

Prep Time: 30 minutes Cook Time: 2 hoiurs Total Time: 3 hours Difficulty: Hard Source: Basics with Babish

Ingredients

Onion Base


5 Spanish onions

1 cup white wine

2 Tbsp flour


Soup


6 cups Beef stock, Use recipe below to a make a quick version

Fish sauce, optional

Baguette crotons, Bite-size so they’re easier to eat

Mozzarella Cheese, shredded


Quick Stock


Beef Stock Paste, 7 tsp

Water, 7 cups

1 packet gelatin

1 Onion, Quartered

2 Carrot, Quartered

2 Celery, Quartered

1 garlic bulb, Sliced in half

1 bunch parsley

2 bay leaves

Sprinkle of peppercorns

Parmesan cheese rind, Optional

Directions

Stock

Prepare your stock. Ideally you would use homemade beef stock, but you can also “soup” up a store bought version. To do that, add your beef broth to a stock pot and bring to a simmer. Add aromatics like parsley, sliced carrots, thyme sprigs, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns, cloves, and parmesan rind. Let it simmer for 1 hour.

Onions

In a large dutch oven drizzle 2-4 Tbsp olive oil. Place this near the onions.

Peel onions and then cut in half from pole to pole - through the root and stem. Place the cut onions into the dutch oven. It will look like way too many, but just trust me.

Place the dutch oven onto the stove top and slowly caramelize the onions over medium heat. Keep them moving constantly, and scrape down the sides of the pot.

The onions are done when they are soft, jammy, and caramelized with a lot of fond on the bottom of the pot. This will take almost an hour.

Add 2 Tbsp flour to the onions and cook for 1-2 minutes.

Deglaze the pot with 1 cup of white wine and cook, scraping up fond from the bottom of the pot.

Combine

Once the alcohol is cooked oﬀ, add the beef broth. Make sure to strain it first if you added aromatics.

Let the whole thing simmer for 5-10 minutes to let the flavors get to know each other.

Optional: add umami boosters. I like to add a splash of fish sauce and soy sauce to richen the flavors.

Crotons

Cut thick slices of day-old french baguette into bite-sized pieces. Put them on a baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and top with salt and pepper.

Toast bread in a 400°F oven for 5 minutes.

Slice a clove of garlic in half and rub down each piece of bread with the garlic.

Plate

Ladle soup into broiler-safe cups. Top with sliced baguette and shredded cheese.

Put the bowls onto a baking sheet and put into an oven preheated to broil. Broil for 3-5 minutes until golden brown.
