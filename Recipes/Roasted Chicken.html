Roasted Chicken

★★★★

Protein, Weekends

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 1 hour 15 minutes Total Time: 1 hour 45 minutes Difficulty: Medium Servings: 4 servings Source: Chow.com

Ingredients

1 whole chicken

1 tbsp olive oil

salt

black pepper

1 lemon

fresh poultry herbs

Directions

Heat the oven to 425°F and arrange a rack in the middle

Place the chicken on a work surface or cutting board and pat it dry with paper towels

Cut off and discard any extra fat hanging around the body cavity

Drizzle the oil on the chicken and rub it all over the skin

Season generously inside and out with salt and pepper

Place the lemon and herbs inside the cavity, if using

Place the chicken breast-side up in a large frying pan or cast-iron skillet

Roast the chicken in the oven for 15 minutes

Reduce the temperature to 375°F and continue roasting until the juices run clear and a thermometer inserted into the inner thigh (but not touching the bone) registers 165°F, about 50 minutes to 1 hour more

Remove the chicken from the oven and place on a cutting board

Let it rest about 15 to 20 minutes before carving
