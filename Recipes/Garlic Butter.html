Garlic Butter

★★★★★

Marinades, Rubs

Difficulty: Easy Source: All Recipes

Ingredients

1 cup unsalted butter, softened

1 Tbsp minced garlic, or more to taste

¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese

1 tsp garlic salt, or more to taste

1 tsp Italian seasoning, or more to taste

½ tsp ground black pepper, or more to taste

¼ tsp ground paprika, or more to taste

Directions

1. Combine softened butter, Parmesan cheese, and minced garlic in a bowl until well combined. Mix in garlic salt, Italian seasoning, pepper, and paprika until smooth and well combined.

2. Taste and adjust seasonings.
