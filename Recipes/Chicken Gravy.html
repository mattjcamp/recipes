Chicken Gravy

★★★★

Sauces, Sides

Difficulty: Easy Servings: 6 Source: M.allrecipes.com

Ingredients

2 tbsp flour

1.5 cup stock

drippings from chicken roast

Directions

Remove the Chicken:

Once your roast chicken is done, transfer it to a cutting board to rest.

Pour off the pan drippings into a measuring cup. Let it sit a minute to separate the fat from the juices.

Deglaze the Pan (optional but recommended):

Put the roasting pan on the stove over medium heat.

Add a splash of wine or stock and scrape up all the brown bits stuck to the bottom with a wooden spoon.

Make a Roux:

In a saucepan (or the roasting pan if you're using it directly), add about 2 tablespoons of the chicken fat.

Stir in the flour and cook over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until it forms a golden paste (this is your roux).

Add Liquid:

Gradually whisk in the remaining pan juices (minus excess fat) and enough chicken stock to get your desired gravy amount.

Stir constantly to avoid lumps.

Simmer:

Bring to a simmer and cook until the gravy thickens, about 5–7 minutes.

Taste and season with salt and pepper. Add a splash of cream for richness or herbs for extra flavor, if desired.

Tips:

If your roast didn’t produce much juice, just use more stock.

You can strain the gravy at the end for a smoother texture.

If it's too thin, simmer longer; too thick, add more stock or water.
