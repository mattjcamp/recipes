Flat Bread Tortillas

★★

Guides

Prep Time: 10 Cook Time: 5 Total Time: 15 Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

100g flour

5g salt

10g neutral oil

35g water

Directions

Combine flour and salt

Add oil and stir until well combined.

Add a few tablespoons of warm water until the dough can be gathered into a ball.

Quickly knead dough and then let hydrate for 15 minutes

Knead dough until you get a smooth ball

Let rest 30 minutes

Use tortilla press to flatten dough, moving 25%.

Cook each tortilla on an ungreased skillet over medium-high heat for about 1 minute (30 seconds on each side) until puffy.
