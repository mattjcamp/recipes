Italian Hoagie

★★★★

Prep

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Difficulty: Easy Servings: 6 servings Source: bakerbynature.com

Ingredients

hoagie roll


salami

soprassata

ham

capicola

prosciutto

provolone


1 tomato thinly sliced

1 onion thinly sliced

1/2 cup iceberg lettuce finely shredded


2 tbsp red wine vinegar

1 teaspoon dried oregano

4 tbsp olive oil


Directions

Slice the rolls lengthwise, cutting only half way through

In a small bowl whisk together the vinegar and oregano; slowly add in the oil, whisking as you pour to emulsify the mixture.

Drizzle a small amount of the oil/vinegar mixture on the rolls, then layer the cheese and meats.

Dress the lettuce with the dressing

Top with the tomatoes, onions, lettuce, then peppers. Drizzle with additional oil/vinegar, cut in half, and serve!
