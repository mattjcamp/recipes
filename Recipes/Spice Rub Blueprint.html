Spice Rub Blueprint

★★★

Marinades, Rubs

Difficulty: Easy Source: Charbroil

Ingredients

Base


15g salt, 1 part

8g pepper (black, chili, cayenne), 1/2 part

15g garlic powder, 1 part but can be generous

15g onion powder, 1 part but can be generous


Base Flavor


45g paprika, 3 part but can be generous


Italian


8g oregano, 1/2 part

8g basil, 1/2 part

8g thyme, 1/2 part


Mediterranean


8g cumin, 1/2 part

8g coriander, 1/2 part

8g sumac, 1/2 part

8g cinnamon, 1/2 part

8g cardamom, 1/2 part


Asian


23g Chinese Five Spice, 1 1/2 parts

Directions

Mix herbs and spices, transfer to a jar

Use for chicken, lamb, pork and minced/ground meats

Mix a few teaspoons with lemon/lime juice, olive oil and salt to taste and mix that with the type of meat you selected

For kebabs or burgers, add to the mince with some salt
