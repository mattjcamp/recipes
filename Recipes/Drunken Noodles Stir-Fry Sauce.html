Drunken Noodles Stir-Fry Sauce

★★★★

Noodles, Sauces

Prep Time: 30 minutes Cook Time: 15 minutes Total Time: 1 hour Difficulty: Hard Servings: Servings: 1 Source: Carls Bad Cravings

Ingredients

2 tbsp oyster sauce

2 tbsp reduced sodium soy sauce

1 tbsp dark brown sugar

1 tbsp rice vinegar

1 tsp molasses

1/2 tbsp fish sauce

2 tsp chili paste

1/2 tsp ground coriander

1/2 tsp ground ginger

1/4 tsp pepper

4 chopped Thai peppers

Directions

Prepare Sauce

- Whisk all of the ingredients together in a medium bowl
- Make it a spicy drunken noodle sauce by adding Thai peppers or a very spicy sauce

NOTE I reduced the main liquid to try and make the dish less saucy
