Sandwich Bread

★★★★★

Bread

Difficulty: Medium Source: Brian Lagerstrom

Ingredients

Pre-Ferment


1 tbsp sourdough discard, optional, can substitute yeast

pinch yeast, optional, can substitute sourdough discard

65g water

65g unbleached flour


Dough


125g pre-ferment

350g warm water

20g olive oil

530g bread flour

3g instant yeast

12g sugar

11g salt


Directions

Pre-Ferment

Combine ingredients for the type of pre-ferment you are using

Leave out on counter overnight

Hydrate (15 minutes)

Combine everything in a bowl

Squeeze and pinch with a wet hand or dough whisk.

Stretch & Fold (2 hours, 30 minute folds)

Flip into a bowl and allow to rise at room temp for 2 hours doing slap and folds every 30 minutes for a total of three times

Proof (1 hour)

Prepare 1.5lb/10"x5.5"x3" loaf pan by liberally oiling bottom and sides with olive oil.

Flip dough onto a floured work surface

De-gas with fingertips

Shape loaf by folding three of the corners as you would a sourdough Boole. Starting with the fourth side roll it up like a cigar and seal each fold.

Transfer to prepared loaf pan seam side down, cover, and allow to rise at room temp for 1 hour

Bake (45 minutes)

Diagonally score 4-5 times and bake in preheated 425F/218C oven for 40-45min.
