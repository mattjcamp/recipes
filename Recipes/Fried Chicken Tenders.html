Fried Chicken Tenders

★★★★

Protein, Weekends

Prep Time: 4 hours Cook Time: 30 minutes Total Time: 5 hours Difficulty: Medium Source: All Recipes

Ingredients

chicken, thin-sliced


Marinade


2 cups buttermilk

2 tbsp salt

1 tsp sugar

1 1/5 tsp MSG

1 tbsp garlic powder

2 tsp smoke paprika

1 tsp cayenne pepper


Dredge


2 1/2 cups flour

1/2 cup corn starch

1 tbsp salt

1 tsp MSG

1 tsp cayenne

1 tsp garlic

1/2 onion powder

2 tsp pepper

1 tsp oregano

Directions

Marinate

- mix marinade
- put chicken into marinade
- refrigerate for at least a few hours or overnight

Bread Chicken

- mix flour
- add 1/3 cup mixture to marinade
- flick in a few drops of marinade into flour
- put each chicken into marinade
- press flour mixture into chicken pieces
- let them rest for at least 30 minutes but better for a few hours in the fridge

Cook Chicken

Fill a large skillet about 1/3 to 1/2 full with vegetable oil

Heat to 350F

Put in as many chicken pieces as the skillet can hold. Brown the chicken in HOT oil on both sides.

Drain the fried chicken on paper towels. Depending on how much chicken you have, you may have to fry in a few shifts. Keep the finished chicken in a slightly warm oven while preparing the rest.
